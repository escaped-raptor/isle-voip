Species Specific VoIP (The Isle Evrima)

A click-through demo of what you get, using made-up players and a pretend server. Nothing is installed, saved or sent anywhere.

What is pretend here

  • The players, their species, positions and the voice group are made up.
  • No audio is sent or played. "Hold to simulate your voice" moves the microphone meter instead of using a real microphone.
  • Steam sign-in, the certificate and the game's call key are skipped, with a note saying what the real app does.
  • Changes you make last until you leave the page.

Every screen has a "Back to demo menu" button at the bottom.
